# Code of Conduct

AASM should be a place where people can critique ideas and code rigorously without attacking the people contributing them.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive
- assume good intent while still reviewing technical claims critically
- welcome newcomers and questions
- focus disagreement on ideas, evidence, code, and tradeoffs
- acknowledge mistakes and corrections
- respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, or targeted hostility

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, spam, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the project community.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through a private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private method is available, open a minimal issue requesting a private contact channel **without posting sensitive details publicly**.

GitHub's own platform policies and reporting mechanisms also apply to activity hosted on GitHub.
