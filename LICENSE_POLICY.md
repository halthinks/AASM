# AASM Licensing Policy

AASM is licensed under the **Apache License, Version 2.0** (`Apache-2.0`).

## Project-wide Apache-2.0 grant

To the extent the AASM copyright holders and contributors have the legal right to do so, **all AASM source code, documentation, tags, commits, and release versions are offered under Apache-2.0, including AASM versions that were first distributed under the MIT License.**

This is an additional license grant for those earlier AASM versions. It is not limited to versions created after the repository changed its root `LICENSE` file.

Older archives or tags may contain a historical `LICENSE` file that records the terms under which that particular copy was originally published. That historical file does not make the corresponding AASM version MIT-only: the same AASM material is also offered under Apache-2.0 under this project-wide licensing declaration, to the extent AASM has the necessary relicensing rights.

## Earlier MIT grants remain valid

Nothing in this policy attempts to revoke permissions that were already granted to recipients under the MIT License. A recipient who lawfully received an AASM version under MIT may continue to rely on that grant.

The continuing validity of an earlier MIT grant does **not** change AASM's current project-wide declared license policy and does **not** prevent AASM from additionally offering the same copyrighted material under Apache-2.0.

## Current distributions

Current AASM distributions include:

- the full Apache License 2.0 text in `LICENSE`;
- AASM attribution in `NOTICE`;
- PEP 639/SPDX package metadata declaring `Apache-2.0`;
- `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` in packaged distributions.

## Third-party material

This policy applies only to material AASM has the right to license or relicense. Any third-party material remains subject to its own applicable license terms and notices.

## Interpretation

The intended public statement is:

> **AASM is Apache-2.0 across the project, including prior AASM versions to the extent AASM has the rights to relicense them. Previously granted MIT permissions remain valid for their recipients, but prior AASM versions are not designated MIT-only.**

This file describes AASM's licensing declaration. It is not a substitute for legal advice regarding third-party contributions or material for which AASM does not control relicensing rights.
